# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free
environment for everyone participating in this project, regardless of
background, identity, experience, or level of expertise.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and considerate.
- Discuss ideas and technical decisions without personal attacks.
- Welcome questions and differing perspectives.
- Give and receive feedback with empathy.
- Respect privacy and research-data confidentiality.

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, unwanted sexual attention, and the
publication of another person's private information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when behavior violates this policy.

To report conduct concerns privately, use the repository's private reporting
channel:

<https://github.com/tkunerlab/calcium-imaging-dashboard/security/advisories/new>

Reports will be reviewed fairly and kept confidential to the extent practical.
